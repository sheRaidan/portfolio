About Me

I believe that the key to strong design is the human aspect; people know how to make for people. A design should have feeling and explore new opportunities. I know that I can’t always communicate my thoughts well, so I strive to design in a way that makes the world easier to understand.

I always ask for feedback on my work from other designers and average people. I get so bogged down in the details that I have to ensure others understand the big picture. It’s hard to accept criticism, but that’s the only way to ensure your work is the best it can be.
